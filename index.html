About Me

Hello My name is Willy, i am an Informatics undergraduate with a strong interest in Data Analytics. Passionate about transforming raw data into actionable insights, I have experience in data visualization, reporting, dashboard development, and business analysis using tools such as Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI, and Looker Studio. Through various analytics projects, I have developed interactive dashboards for HR Analytics, Marketplace Growth Analytics, and Sales & Profit Analysis to identify trends, optimize business performance, and support data driven, decision making.

My background in product design, marketing performance, and IT support has also strengthened my analytical thinking, attention to detail, problem-solving abilities, and data storytelling skills enabling me to present insights in a clear and impactful way. Currently, I am continuously improving my technical and analytical skills in data analysis, business intelligence, and visualization, while actively seeking opportunities to contribute and grow as a Data Analyst or Business Intelligence professional.
